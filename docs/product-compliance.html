What adult apparel, footwear, bags, fashion jewellery and accessories must meet to be sold to consumers in each destination, which return rights the buyer has, and what we are liable for as the seller. What we found

Who owes it

The duties land on us, not the merchant

As seller, and usually importer, we are the party the law reaches: confirmed for AU, PH, SG, the US, Canada and, with a Dutch BV, the EU. The merchant's duties to us are contractual.

Key decision

Who is importer of record

If we are, we certify and eFile in the US, are the EU responsible person (Dutch BV) and the strictly liable importer in most markets. If the buyer is, as with Global-e, some of this falls away.

Returns

Keeping duties on returns is unlawful in key markets

A withdrawal in the EU, UK, Norway and Taiwan (and probably Korea) refunds everything paid, duties included. Every market requires a full refund for defects.

Catalogue

Gate a few categories at launch

Exclude children's clothing, exotic leathers, real fur. Gate sunglasses per market. Jewellery and leather need lab reports. No bare "gold" or "silver".

Cotton

US forced-labour risk

Vietnamese garments often use Chinese yarn. Under UFLPA the importer proves cotton origin from bale to garment. The EU follows from 14-Dec-2027.

Heaviest markets

EU and US need the most setup

EU: responsible person, REACH incl. PFAS, producer registrations, withdrawal button. US: CPSC eFiling per variant since 08-Jul-2026, "imported" on every page, Prop 65, state PFAS bans.

6 · Liability and the merchant contract

Global-e's split

The merchant warrants compliance, labelling, origin and IP, owns product-data errors, indemnifies including product liability, and carries worldwide insurance. Refunds including duties and chargebacks come off payouts. Global-e caps its own liability at 12 months of fees.

Our additions

Arbitration in Singapore (enforceable in VN); a 5–10% rolling reserve for 90–180 days; merchant cover of at least US$1m with us as additional insured, or a master policy; traceability answers within 5 business days; indemnity surviving 10 years.

What we do ourselves

The consumer contract and all statutory remedies, merchant identity checks, category gating, lab reports, counterfeit screening, recall monitoring, incident deadlines (AU 2 days, CA 2 and 10 days), and our own product liability policy (US$1–2m, estimate only).
