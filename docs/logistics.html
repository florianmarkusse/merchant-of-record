Logistics, customs and returns

Which couriers can carry parcels from our Vietnamese merchants to buyers in SEA and the rest of the world, what that costs and how fast it is, and what each destination's customs rules ask of us as the seller. Summary of docs/research-logistics.md; every source is in docs/research/logistics/. Nothing here is decided yet, and most prices need sales quotes.

What we found

Reach

One integrator reaches about 220 countries

DHL Express, FedEx and UPS all ship duty-paid from VN with a REST API. Where a merchant can sell is limited by our tax registrations and product rules, not by couriers.

Price

Express is too expensive for SEA orders

A 0.5 kg parcel to SEA costs about US$58–64 at list price, plus 50% fuel (DHL) and a duty-paid fee of about US$23. SEA needs a consolidated line at an estimated US$4–9.

SEA lines

The cheap options from VN have thinned out

Ninja Van (2025), DHL eCommerce (2021) and LEX (2026) left VN. What remains (Janio, Boxme, J&T) sells DDP only on contract, with thin API documentation.

Orchestrators

No help from VN today

Easyship, EasyPost, Shippo and ShipStation sell no VN rates; they work only on our own DHL, FedEx or UPS accounts. Revisit them when merchants outside VN join.

Rules

Low-value exemptions are closing

TH has no de minimis since 2026, the US since 2025 with +12.5% on VN goods, the EU charges €3 per item plus €2, the UK ends its duty relief by 2028 and JP changes its rules in 2028. The duty now falls on us.

Indonesia

On hold until an adviser looks at it

A US$100 minimum price per unit for direct cross-border sales, 25% duty plus taxes on apparel (about +45–50% in total), and a representative required above 1,000 parcels a year.

High-value goods; outside SEAIntegrator return label straight to VN~$15–40+ (est.)

We refund the goods and the tax we collected ourselves (by credit note or an adjustment on the next return). Duty paid at the border may be kept only on voluntary change-of-mind returns where that was disclosed before purchase. A withdrawal in the EU, UK, Norway, Taiwan and probably Korea, and every refund for defective goods, must include duties and tax (corrected 06-Nov-2026; see the product compliance research).

6 · Open before we decide

  • Quotes in the same week: Janio, Boxme, J&T; DHL, FedEx, UPS Vietnam; Asendia, YunExpress. Ask for rates for the five reference parcels, DDP with duties billed to us, the duty invoice format, our tax IDs on the label, API docs and webhook signing.
  • Can we skip the US$23 duty-paid fee on lanes where nothing is collected at the border (SG and MY once registered, AU, NZ, UK, NO, HK)?
  • Entity and exporter of record: can an SG or NL entity hold VN export accounts, and whose name goes in the shipper field? A compliance question; do not assume the answer.
  • Advisers: ID minimum price and representative; TH duty plan; PH VAT below the threshold; MY per-item test; VN export VAT and re-import VAT; EU entity choice; US importer of record and the Section 301 decision due 29-Nov-2026; JP registration for 2028.
  • Personal data: KR PCCC, ID NIK, IN KYC and CN IDs fall under local privacy laws.
