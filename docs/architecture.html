3 · One payment, end to end

  1. The buyer submits the payment form.
  2. One transaction: the attempt row (created) and an outbox command. The handler returns at once.
  3. A worker claims the command.
  4. It calls the PSP with the idempotency key stored for this attempt. On a timeout the attempt becomes outcome_unknown. The worker retries with the same key only inside that PSP's window (1 h at Mollie up to 14 days at Adyen). Otherwise it looks the payment up by our reference first. It never fails over to another PSP.
  5. The PSP answers that the buyer has to act (redirect URL or QR payload).
  6. The worker commits that state.
  7. The checkout page shows the QR or redirects. Status refreshes by a plain page reload, so no script is needed.
  8. The buyer pays in their bank or wallet app.
  9. The webhook is verified, stored raw in the inbox with a unique event key, and acknowledged with 200. Nothing else happens in that request.
  10. A worker claims the inbox row.
  11. It treats the webhook as a hint and fetches the real state, because order is not guaranteed and some webhooks carry only an id.
  12. The PSP confirms capture.
  13. One transaction: attempt captured, the ledger entry, and the outbox event.
  14. The merchant receives a signed payment_captured webhook (versioned schema). In the same way, a courier shipment is booked from this event.
