# Plan: markets and logistics data

Status: agreed 2026-11-07, first version built. The data lives in
`docs/markets/`. The views are generated by `scripts/markets.py`.

## Why

The logistics and product-compliance research (`research-logistics.md`,
`research-product-compliance.md`, `research/`) is prose. To pick
countries and carriers we need the same facts as tables: one place per
fact, with where it came from and how sure we are, and a clear mark where
a value waits on a re-fetch, an email or an adviser.

## Decisions

- **CSV files in the repo are the source of truth** (decided 2026-11-07:
  only the logistics lead and Claude edit for now, and git history shows
  how each fact changed). The `.xlsx` for Google Drive and the HTML page
  are generated from them and never edited by hand. The `.xlsx` has one
  sheet per CSV with the same columns, so edits made in Drive can later be
  read back into the CSVs. That import is not built yet; it is built when
  someone first edits in Drive.
- **One row per fact, not per country** (decided 2026-11-07: sources and
  certainty differ per fact). The wide country overview is generated.
- **All ISO 3166-1 countries are listed** with a market status; only the
  researched ones (about 45) have facts. More are researched as merchants'
  buyers call for them.
- **Facts can be set for a group** (EU, ASEAN, GCC) and overridden per
  country. The most specific row wins. Two groups giving the same fact for
  one country is an error.
- **Origin is a column** (decided 2026-11-07: only VN for now, other SEA
  origins later). Facts that depend on it (FTA preference) and every
  carrier service carry it.
- **A fixed list of product categories** in `categories.csv`: apparel,
  leather footwear, ceramics, keycaps, lacquerware, wooden goods. A fact
  with no category applies to all. Adding a category is one row.
- **Carrier prices are split** into services, rate cards, zones, rates and
  surcharges, because each changes on its own (fuel monthly, rate cards
  yearly, zones rarely). Each rate card keeps its own weight breaks and
  its own currency (VN cards are in VND). Amounts are integers in the
  currency's minor unit, as in AGENTS.md Money and Ledger.
- **The files live in `docs/markets/`, not `data/`** (decided 2026-11-07:
  `data/` is gitignored for real merchant and buyer data; this is public
  research and belongs with the other research documents).
- **Sources are inline** on each row (`source`, `source_url`,
  `research_ref`), not in a separate table, so a hand edit needs no
  lookup.
- **Provider documents go in `docs/markets/sources/<provider>/`**, named
  after the provider id (decided 2026-11-07: one place per provider for
  rate guides, zone charts and quote emails). The documents are
  gitignored because carrier rate files are often for registered
  customers only; each folder's committed `README.md` lists the files and
  where to get them.
- **All-in cost is computed, not typed** (decided 2026-11-07: a total per
  carrier, destination and reference parcel changes whenever fuel or a fee
  changes, so it is derived from the tables). Inputs: `scenarios.csv` (the
  reference parcels: weight, goods value, category), `border_charges.csv`
  (when a destination collects duty or tax at the border, with us
  registered in its seller scheme where one exists) and three surcharge
  columns: `trigger` (`always`, `duties_paid`, `passthrough`, `optional`,
  `on_event`), `destinations` (blank = all) and `fuel_applies`. The total
  counts freight, fuel, `always` fees, and `duties_paid` fees where the
  border rule says duty or tax is due. Duties and taxes themselves, and
  pass-through duties such as VIO's EUR 3, are left out because they are
  the same whichever carrier carries the parcel. A missing amount makes the
  total a lower bound (shown as ≥), never zero.
- **Display rates are ECB reference rates** (decided 2026-11-07: an
  official, dated source for every currency we meet; VND stays at the
  research estimate of 26,300 because the ECB does not publish it). They
  live in `ECB_PER_EUR` in the script with their date; refresh them with
  the fuel values before a comparison.
- **Open work is in `questions.csv`**: emails to carriers, adviser
  questions and re-fetches. A fact waiting on one has status
  `awaiting_answer` and the question id.
- **Every open question has a priority, and names its providers**
  (decided 2026-11-10: the list had grown to 40 and nothing said what to
  chase first). `priority` is `now` (needed for the next decisions),
  `before_launch` (before the first live order) or `later` (later
  markets); `due_on` is the reply or follow-up date; `blocked_by` says
  what stops it being sent; `providers` holds the ids from
  `providers.csv` it goes to, space-separated. `check` fails on an open
  question without a priority or with an unknown provider.
  `providers.csv` `relationship` is a closed set: `none`, `rfq_planned`,
  `rfq_sent`, `quoted`, `account_planned`, `account_open`, `contracted`,
  `not_pursued`, `n/a`. The views add **To do** (open questions by
  priority, then due date) and **Contacts** (one row per provider: the
  questions sent, the last send date and the next step).

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | read in a primary source (authority, gazette, carrier rate card) |
| `secondary` | adviser, law firm, carrier marketing, press or aggregator |
| `estimate` | our inference, a rating, a conversion or a third-party estimate |
| `unverified` | a value we have but could not confirm, or sources conflict |
| `to_fetch` | no value yet; can be looked up on the web |
| `awaiting_answer` | no reliable value; waits on the linked question |
| `n/a` | does not apply |

## Files

| File | One row per |
|---|---|
| `countries.csv` | ISO 3166-1 country: codes, name, UN region, currency, market status |
| `groups.csv` | group (EU, ASEAN, GCC, research regions) and its members |
| `categories.csv` | product category |
| `topics.csv` | fact topic: what a column of the overview means |
| `facts.csv` | fact: country or group × topic × category × origin |
| `questions.csv` | question: email, adviser question or re-fetch, with priority, due date, blocker and providers |
| `providers.csv` | logistics or landed-cost provider and our contact status |
| `services.csv` | carrier service from an origin |
| `rate_cards.csv` | price list of a service: kind, currency, validity, whether fuel and VAT are included |
| `zones.csv` | country or group → zone, per rate card, with the mapping's status |
| `rates.csv` | price per rate card × zone × weight |
| `surcharges.csv` | fee per service, or per rate card where a card has its own terms |
| `transit.csv` | transit days per service × destination |
| `service_restrictions.csv` | what a service allows per product category |
| `scenarios.csv` | reference parcel for the all-in cost: weight, goods value, category |
| `border_charges.csv` | country or group (× category): duty or tax collected at the border always, never, or above a limit |

## Commands

```
uv run scripts/markets.py check   # validate all CSVs
uv run scripts/markets.py build   # check, then write the views
```

`build` writes `docs/markets.html` and `docs/markets/out/markets.xlsx`
(not committed; upload it to Drive).

## Next: quote terms and the landed-cost breakdown

Status: designed and agreed 14-Nov-2026. Three phases, each
built and checked before the next.

### Why

Loading J&T's quote (q06, 14-Nov-2026) showed five weak spots:

- Surcharges hang off a service, so a quote with its own terms needed
  fake services (`jt_intl_quote`, `jt_intl_quote_sg_fast`), a made-up 0%
  fuel row and duplicated fees.
- What a price includes is free text (`excludes`) that the comparison
  never reads: J&T's quote includes VAT, the integrators' cards do not.
- Nothing says which offer is current: the 2024 J&T card and the 2026
  quote are both compared.
- Assumed zone mappings (AU → J&T zone 1, FICP zones = IP zones) are
  in `notes` only, so their totals show as verified.
- Price drops with weight (J&T's JP and US at 5 kg) are found by eye.

Duties and import taxes are also left out of the all-in total, on the
grounds that they are the same for every carrier. That is only roughly
true: the duty-handling fee is a share of the duties, some countries tax
goods plus freight (MY, KR, CH), and on a duties-unpaid (DAP) service the
buyer pays duty and a collection fee at the door, which a merchant of
record must avoid. We want one breakdown per carrier service ×
destination × reference parcel: freight, fuel, fixed surcharges,
duty-handling fee, duty, import tax, total landed cost.

### Phase 1: terms per rate card, inclusions as fixed values, zone status

Built 14-Nov-2026. Besides the plan below: `fuel` also takes `unknown`
(VN Post's calculator and VIO Extra do not say); the J&T 2024 card's
surcharges moved onto that card, so the quote does not inherit them; VIO's
and the sea-line estimate's 0% fuel rows became `fuel=included`; J&T's
goods rules were copied to `jt_intl_sg_fast`; FedEx cards whose PDFs do not
state VAT are `vat=unknown`; the VAT question went into the Vietnam
adviser question q15.

**A. Surcharges per rate card.** `surcharges.csv` gets a `rate_card`
column. Blank means every card of the service; a value limits the row to
that card. For a card, the card's own rows for a surcharge name replace
the service rows of that name. Migration:

- `jt_intl_vn_2026_quote` moves to service `jt_intl`; its card-level
  remote-area and delivery-day fees move with it, and service
  `jt_intl_quote` and its 0% fuel row are removed.
- The Singapore 2-3 days column stays a service of its own (a faster
  product, with its own transit), renamed `jt_intl_sg_fast`. Its four
  fees stay on it: cheap duplication of a different product.
- `jt_intl` becomes `ddp=no`, as the quote says duties are unpaid.

**B. Inclusions as fixed values.** `rate_cards.csv` replaces `excludes`
with:

- `fuel`: `included` | `extra`. `included` makes the fuel line read
  "bundled in freight" instead of needing a 0% fuel row.
- `vat`: `included` | `excluded` | `unknown`. The total keeps the price
  as quoted and carries an "incl. VAT" mark. Whether Vietnamese VAT on
  export freight is 0% or 10%, and whether we could reclaim it, goes to
  the adviser as a new question; until it is answered we do not adjust.

The rest of the old `excludes` text (discount, duties) moves to `notes`.

**D. Zone status.** `zones.csv` gets a `status` column. Existing rows are
`verified`, except the assumptions (J&T AU → `au_1`, FICP zones from the
IP column), which become `estimate`. A total takes the worst status of
its parts, its zone included.

### Phase 2: the landed-cost breakdown

**Computable duty and tax rates.** A new `landed_rates.csv`, one row per
country or group × category (blank = all) × charge:

| Column | Values |
|---|---|
| `charge` | `duty`, `import_tax`, `other_tax` (e.g. ID's 5% PPh on apparel) |
| `basis` | `percent`, `per_line`, `per_parcel` |
| `rate_percent` / `amount_minor` + `currency` | the rate or the flat amount |
| `base` | `goods`, `goods_plus_freight`, `goods_freight_duty` (CIF plus duty) |
| `effective_from` | when the rate started |
| status, source columns | as in the other tables |

Most specific row wins, as in `facts.csv`. The `duty_rate` and
`import_tax` facts stay as the readable summary with ranges; this table
holds the single figure we compute with, and its `notes` say which end
of a range was taken. The EU's EUR 3 flat duty moves here from VIO's
`passthrough` surcharge; VIO's EUR 5 customs fee stays a surcharge.

Rates come from the destinations' public tariff and tax sources. No paid
landed-cost API (Dutify, Zonos) is called for this.

**Breakdown per parcel.** For each service × reference destination ×
scenario, lines in this order, each with its status:

1. freight
2. fuel (or "bundled in freight")
3. fixed surcharges (`always`)
4. duty-handling fee, now computed from the duty amount instead of
   taking only the minimum
5. duty
6. import tax and other taxes
7. total landed cost

Each duty or tax line says who pays it: `us_at_checkout` (we collect it
through the country's seller scheme), `us_via_carrier` (a duties-paid
service bills us) or `buyer_at_door` (a duties-unpaid service). The
border rule in `border_charges.csv` decides whether duty is due at all.
Customs values that include freight use freight plus fuel. Amounts stay
exact `Fraction`s and are rounded half up only for display, in USD at the
ECB rates as now.

**Size counts.** `scenarios.csv` gets `length_cm`, `width_cm` and
`height_cm`. The billed weight is the greater of actual and volumetric
weight (L×W×H / the service's `volumetric_divisor`), rounded up to the
card's weight step.

**Views.** `markets.html` gets a breakdown table per destination (rows:
services, columns: the lines above). The `.xlsx` gets a long-format sheet
(service, card, destination, scenario, category, line, amount, currency,
status, paid by) for filtering in Drive.

**Default category: apparel** (decided 14-Nov-2026: duty depends on the
category and showing all six multiplies the table by six; apparel is
where duty weighs most, e.g. ID's 25%). Other categories can be built on
request.

### Phase 3, after phases 1 and 2: offer lifecycle and data checks

**C. Which offer is current.** `rate_cards.csv` gets:

- `received_on`: when we got the price (a quote often has no start date).
- `valid_until`: the end of validity, where given (alongside
  `effective_to`, which is when the carrier says the card ends).
- `supersedes`: the card this one replaces, from the same provider.
- `volume_per_month` (an integer, blank = any) and `account_required`
  (`yes` | `no` | `unknown`) for conditional quotes.

The comparison shows by default only current cards: not superseded, not
past `valid_until` on the build date. Superseded and expired cards are
listed in their own section. `check` fails on a `supersedes` that points
to another provider or forms a cycle.

**E. Checks on entered prices.** `check` fails when a card's price for a
zone falls as weight rises, unless the row's new `ack` column names the
question that asked about it (e.g. `q45`) or is `confirmed`. (A rate for
a zone with no destinations already fails `check`.) A mapped zone with no
rates also fails. `build` also lists, per current card, the reference destinations it does
not cover, so a gap is visible instead of silently missing from a table.

## Project rules applied

- Money and Ledger: amounts as integer minor units with the currency; no
  floats. Rates converted to USD only for display, with the rate shown.
- Merchant of Record Domain Rules: tax rules per jurisdiction with dates
  and sources, so a later module can be seeded from them; compliance and
  licensing points are flagged, not assumed.
- Record Decisions Inline: decisions above carry their date and reason.
