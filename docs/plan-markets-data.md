# Markets and logistics data

Status: built (07-Nov to 18-Nov-2026). The data lives in `docs/markets/`;
the views are generated by `scripts/markets.py`; quoting loads part of it
into the database (`plan-quote.md` §2).

## Why

The logistics and product-compliance research (`research-logistics.md`,
`research-product-compliance.md`, `research/`) is prose. To pick
countries and carriers we need the same facts as tables: one place per
fact, with where it came from and how sure we are, and a clear mark where
a value waits on a re-fetch, an email or an adviser.

## Decisions

- **CSV files in the repo are the source of truth** (decided 07-Nov-2026:
  only the logistics lead and Claude edit for now, and git history shows
  how each fact changed). The `.xlsx` for Google Drive and the HTML page
  are generated from them and never edited by hand. The `.xlsx` has one
  sheet per CSV with the same columns, so edits made in Drive can later be
  read back into the CSVs. That import is not built yet; it is built when
  someone first edits in Drive.
- **One row per fact, not per country** (decided 07-Nov-2026: sources and
  certainty differ per fact). The wide country overview is generated.
- **All ISO 3166-1 countries are listed** with a market status; only the
  researched ones (about 45) have facts. More are researched as merchants'
  buyers call for them.
- **Facts can be set for a group** (EU, ASEAN, GCC) and overridden per
  country. The most specific row wins. Two groups giving the same fact for
  one country is an error.
- **Origin is a column** (decided 07-Nov-2026: only VN for now, other SEA
  origins later). Facts that depend on it (FTA preference) and every
  carrier service carry it.
- **A fixed list of product categories** in `categories.csv`: apparel,
  leather footwear, ceramics, keycaps, lacquerware, wooden goods. A fact
  with no category applies to all. Adding a category is one row.
- **Carrier prices are split** into services, rate cards, zones, rates and
  surcharges, because each changes on its own (fuel monthly, rate cards
  yearly, zones rarely). Each rate card keeps its own weight breaks and
  its own currency (VN cards are in VND). Amounts are integers in the
  currency's minor unit, as in AGENTS.md Money and Ledger.
- **The files live in `docs/markets/`, not `data/`** (decided 07-Nov-2026:
  `data/` is gitignored for real merchant and buyer data; this is public
  research and belongs with the other research documents).
- **Sources are inline** on each row (`source`, `source_url`,
  `research_ref`), not in a separate table, so a hand edit needs no
  lookup.
- **Provider documents go in `docs/markets/sources/<provider>/`**, named
  after the provider id (decided 07-Nov-2026: one place per provider for
  rate guides, zone charts and quote emails). The documents are
  gitignored because carrier rate files are often for registered
  customers only; each folder's committed `README.md` lists the files and
  where to get them.
- **All-in cost is computed, not typed** (decided 07-Nov-2026: a total per
  carrier, destination and reference parcel changes whenever fuel or a fee
  changes, so it is derived from the tables). Inputs: `scenarios.csv` (the
  reference parcels: weight, goods value, category), `border_charges.csv`
  (when a destination collects duty or tax at the border, with us
  registered in its seller scheme where one exists) and three surcharge
  columns: `trigger` (`always`, `duties_paid`, `passthrough`, `optional`,
  `on_event`), `destinations` (blank = all) and `fuel_applies`. The total
  counts freight, fuel, `always` fees, and `duties_paid` fees where the
  border rule says duty or tax is due. Duties and taxes themselves, and
  fees a carrier only passes through (VIO's EUR 2, the EU handling fee),
  are left out of this carrier total; the landed-cost breakdown (phase 2
  below) adds them from `landed_rates.csv`. A missing amount makes the
  total a lower bound (shown as ≥), never zero.
- **Display rates are ECB reference rates** (decided 07-Nov-2026: an
  official, dated source for every currency we meet; VND stays at the
  research estimate of 26,300 because the ECB does not publish it). They
  live in `fx_rates.csv` with their date, so the views and quoting use the
  same rates; refresh them with the fuel values before a comparison.
- **Quoting reads the database, loaded from these files** (decided
  17-Nov-2026, `docs/plan-quote.md` §2): `uv run python -m
  mor.markets.load` shows what changed and `--write` stores a new set.
  Of these files it loads `categories`, `countries`, `groups`,
  `landed_rates`, `registrations`, `fx_rates`, `hs_rates` and
  `duty_sources`.
- **Open work is in `questions.csv`**: emails to carriers, adviser
  questions and re-fetches. A fact waiting on one has status
  `awaiting_answer` and the question id.
- **Every open question has a priority, and names its providers**
  (decided 10-Nov-2026: the list had grown to 40 and nothing said what to
  chase first). `priority` is `now` (needed for the next decisions),
  `before_launch` (before the first live order) or `later` (later
  markets); `due_on` is the reply or follow-up date; `blocked_by` says
  what stops it being sent; `providers` holds the ids from
  `providers.csv` it goes to, space-separated. `check` fails on an open
  question without a priority or with an unknown provider.
  `providers.csv` `relationship` is a closed set: `none`, `rfq_planned`,
  `rfq_sent`, `quoted`, `account_planned`, `account_open`, `contracted`,
  `not_pursued`, `n/a`. The views add **To do** (open questions by
  priority, then due date) and **Contacts** (one row per provider: the
  questions sent, the last send date and the next step).

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `verified` | read in a primary source (authority, gazette, carrier rate card) |
| `secondary` | adviser, law firm, carrier marketing, press or aggregator |
| `estimate` | our inference, a rating, a conversion or a third-party estimate |
| `unverified` | a value we have but could not confirm, or sources conflict |
| `to_fetch` | no value yet; can be looked up on the web |
| `awaiting_answer` | no reliable value; waits on the linked question |
| `n/a` | does not apply |

## Files

| File | One row per |
|---|---|
| `countries.csv` | ISO 3166-1 country: codes, name, UN region, currency, market status |
| `groups.csv` | group (EU, ASEAN, GCC, research regions) and its members |
| `categories.csv` | product category |
| `topics.csv` | fact topic: what a column of the overview means |
| `facts.csv` | fact: country or group × topic × category × origin |
| `questions.csv` | question: email, adviser question or re-fetch, with priority, due date, blocker and providers |
| `providers.csv` | logistics or landed-cost provider and our contact status |
| `services.csv` | carrier service from an origin; its `carrier` is a `providers.csv` id |
| `rate_cards.csv` | price list of a service: kind, currency, validity, when received, what it supersedes, its conditions, whether fuel and VAT are included |
| `zones.csv` | country or group → zone, per rate card, with the mapping's status |
| `rates.csv` | price per rate card × zone × weight; `ack` on a price that falls as weight rises |
| `surcharges.csv` | fee per service, or per rate card where a card has its own terms; `valid_until` is a weekly fuel rate's last day: a quote past it still uses the rate and flags it (plan-quote §4) |
| `transit.csv` | transit days per service × destination (`check` refuses a minimum above the maximum: a price group's number once ran into the minimum, "EU 1 8–14 days" read as 18–14) |
| `service_restrictions.csv` | what a service allows per product category |
| `scenarios.csv` | reference parcel for the all-in cost: weight, box size, goods value, category |
| `landed_rates.csv` | duty, customs fee or tax per country or group (× category), with its band (always, up to or above a limit), who collects it and its base |
| `border_charges.csv` | country or group (× category): duty or tax collected at the border always, never, or above a limit |
| `fx_rates.csv` | exchange rate: base, quote, exact rate, date, status, source (the ECB reference rates per EUR, and the VND estimate per USD); read by the script for its USD display and loaded for quoting |
| `hs_rates.csv` | a duty or tax rate for products whose HS code starts with a prefix, into a destination or group, from an origin or any; with an agreement and its conditions for a preferential rate (plan-quote §3) |
| `duty_sources.csv` | who decides a destination's rates per HS code: `vendor` (the named vendor's answer, our rows compared with it), `own_first` (our rows, the vendor fills gaps) or `own_only`; a destination not listed is `own_only` (plan-quote §3) |
| `shipping_routes.csv` | which rate card is offered into a country or group, its price source (`own_table`; `carrier_api` in 4c-2) and its standing (`placeholder` until a carrier contract; prod refuses to quote on one) (plan-quote §4) |
| `fees.csv` | our fee components (`psp_fee`, `fx`, `mor_margin`) per buyer country, group or default (blank), from a date: a percentage of the merchant's price plus an optional fixed amount, `placeholder` or `agreed` (plan-quote §4) |
| `registrations.csv` | one of our seller-scheme registrations: country, scheme, number, from when; empty until we register |

## Commands

```
uv run scripts/markets.py check   # validate all CSVs
uv run scripts/markets.py build   # check, then write the views
```

`build` writes `docs/markets.html` and `docs/markets/out/markets.xlsx`
(not committed; upload it to Drive).

## Quote terms and the landed-cost breakdown

Built 14-Nov-2026. Why: loading J&T's quote (q06) showed that surcharges hung off a service (so a quote with
its own terms needed fake services and a made-up 0% fuel row), that what
a price includes was free text the comparison never read, that nothing
said which offer was current, and that assumed zone mappings showed as
verified. Duties and import taxes were also left out of the all-in
total, which is only roughly fair across carriers: the duty-handling fee
is a share of the duties, some countries tax goods plus freight (MY, KR,
CH), and on a duties-unpaid service the buyer pays at the door, which a
merchant of record must avoid.

**Terms per rate card.**
- `surcharges.csv` has a `rate_card` column: blank means every card of
  the service; a card's own rows for a surcharge name replace the
  service's rows of that name. J&T's quote is a card of `jt_intl`
  (`ddp=no`); its faster Singapore column is a service of its own,
  `jt_intl_sg_fast`, with its fees and goods rules copied (cheap
  duplication of a different product).
- `rate_cards.csv` says what a price includes as fixed values instead of
  free text: `fuel` (`included`, `extra`, `unknown`) and `vat`
  (`included`, `excluded`, `unknown`; the total keeps the price as quoted
  with an "incl. VAT" mark). Whether VN VAT on export freight is 0% or
  10%, and whether we can reclaim it, is adviser question q15.
- `zones.csv` has a `status`; assumed mappings (J&T AU → `au_1`, FICP
  zones from the IP column) are `estimate`. A total takes the worst
  status of its parts, its zone included.

**The landed-cost breakdown.**
- `landed_rates.csv`: one row per country or group × category (blank =
  all) × charge (`duty`, `additional_duty` such as US Section 301, `fee`
  such as the EU handling fee, `import_tax`, `other_tax` such as ID's
  income tax), with a basis (`percent`, `per_line`, `per_parcel`), a base
  (`goods`, `goods_plus_freight`, `goods_freight_duty`), a band
  (`applies` = `always`, `up_to` or `above` a limit) and `collected`
  (`border` or `seller`). The most specific row whose band holds the
  parcel's value wins; `check` fails on overlapping bands. Bands live per
  row, not in `border_charges.csv`, because the law draws different lines
  per charge (EU: the EUR 3 duty and EUR 2 fee at the border, VAT through
  IOSS up to EUR 150). Where `border_charges.csv` disagrees (MY), the
  lines are marked unverified. The `duty_rate` and `import_tax` facts stay
  the readable summary with ranges. Rates come from public tariff and tax
  sources, never a paid landed-cost API.
- Per service × reference destination × scenario: freight, fuel (or
  "bundled in freight"), fixed surcharges, the duty-handling fee computed
  from the duty, duty, import and other taxes, total landed cost. Each
  duty or tax line says who pays: `us_at_checkout`, `us_via_carrier` or
  `buyer_at_door`. Customs values that include freight use freight plus
  fuel. Amounts stay exact `Fraction`s, rounded half up only for display,
  in USD at the ECB rates.
- `scenarios.csv` has box sizes; billed weight is the greater of actual
  and volumetric weight, rounded up to the card's step.
- Seeded for apparel only (decided 14-Nov-2026: duty weighs most there,
  e.g. ID's 25%), for the 11 reference destinations, with a cotton
  knitted T-shirt (HS 6109.10). JP's simplified rate and PH's rate are
  `estimate`. Other categories need their own rows.
- VIO's EUR 2 per HS code is the EU handling fee passed through: its
  surcharge is `passthrough`, and the fee counts once, in the landed
  lines.
- `markets.html` has a breakdown table per destination; the `.xlsx` a
  long-format Landed sheet, amounts in integer US cents.

**Which offer is current, and checks on prices.**
- `rate_cards.csv` has `received_on`, `valid_until`, `supersedes`,
  `volume_per_month` and `account_required`. `supersedes` works per
  destination: a newer card replaces an older one only where it has a
  price. The comparison shows current cards; ended and replaced ones are
  listed under Older offers. `check` fails on a `supersedes` to another
  provider or in a cycle.
- `check` fails when a zone's price falls as weight rises, unless the
  row's `ack` names the question that asked about it (J&T's JP and US
  5 kg carry `ack=q45`) or is `confirmed`, and on a mapped zone with no
  rates. `build` lists, per current card, the reference destinations it
  has no price for.

## Project rules applied

- Money and Ledger: amounts as integer minor units with the currency; no
  floats. Rates converted to USD only for display, with the rate shown.
- Merchant of Record Domain Rules: tax rules per jurisdiction with dates
  and sources, so a later module can be seeded from them; compliance and
  licensing points are flagged, not assumed.
- Record Decisions Inline: decisions above carry their date and reason.
